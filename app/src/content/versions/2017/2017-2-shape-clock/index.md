## Story

My initial concept for [#newwwyear](https://twitter.com/jensimmons/status/943305744123916288),
but once I started implementing it I wasn't entirely happy with how it looked and switched to
[Space Calendar](Space%20Calendar%20825ae7255e084572a394ebe10de09b5e.md).

The idea was that the shapes would rotate based on the time of day, and some of the visible shapes
would mimic the current weather.
