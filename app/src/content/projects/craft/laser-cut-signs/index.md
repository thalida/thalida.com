## Hintze Family

![Laser cut wooden sign reading "Life is better at the lake" with an engraved lake scene, "Lake House" banner, and "Hintze Family" nameplate with decorative butterfly and dragonfly accents](laser-cut-signs/hintze-family-lake-house-sign.png)

This sign was created as a gift for family. It features a custom design, and a wood laser engraving of a local lake.
I designed the sign, and it was cut and engraved using a laser cutter.


## Craft Room

![Laser cut wooden sign reading "24/7 Craft Room — cut, print, paint, and more" mounted above a doorway](laser-cut-signs/craft-room-entrance-sign.png)
This sign hanging above the entrance to my craft room. I created the design, and cut it using a laser cutter.

![Laser cut wooden sign reading "Crafter at Work — making an idea into reality" with crossed tools icon, mounted above a door inside the craft room](laser-cut-signs/crafter-at-work-sign.png)
This sign is hanging above the door inside the craft room itself.
It serves as a motivational reminder to try to bring my ideas to life.


## The Coffee Shop

![Circular laser cut wooden sign reading "T&E Coffee Shop — Open 24hrs" with a coffee cup logo, mounted on a honeycomb-patterned wall](laser-cut-signs/coffee-shop-sign.png)

This sign is hanging in a room my partner and I call "The Coffee Shop",
because it has the vibes of a cozy Brooklyn coffee shop. So, I decided to create a sign to memorialize the name.
